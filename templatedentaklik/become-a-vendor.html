Cost to Join

  • Minimal Sign-up fee

    A one-time sign-up fee is due with your contract. This fee is based on the number and type of products you wish to sell on Dentaklik.

  • Monthly commission fee based on actual sales

    Dentaklik will invoice you on the 1st of each month for the commission due on your prior month's sales, so you pay for our services as you receive sales

  • Annual review and renewal fee

    An annual review of your vendor activities and customer service response times is evaluated each year. If you are providing outstanding service as defined in your contract, this fee may be waived.
