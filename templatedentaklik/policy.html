Dentaklik's Website Privacy Policy

Dentaklik, Inc. ("Dentaklik," "we," "us," and "our") is dedicated to providing a marketplace that empowers dentists to share their knowledge about the purchase, care, and use, of dental products and supplies. Our goals are to provide you the very best online service in the dental industry, with lowest possible pricing, industry leading transparency and ease of use. When you visit www.Dentaklik.com or any of our associated web properties such as blog.Dentaklik.com (together the "Site" or "Website"), you may share information with us that helps us achieve these goals. This Privacy Policy ("Policy") is meant to help you understand how we collect, use, and share the information you provide to us when you visit our Website.

The entire Dentaklik team is committed to the responsible collection and use of your information. Our Policy explains:
  • What information we collect and why we collect it;
  • How we use that information; and,
  • The choices you have when you visit our Website and buy products through our marketplace (the "Services").

Please review this Policy as your use of this Website means you have consented to this Policy. You should also read our Terms of Service which set out the contract between you and Dentaklik for the use of our Services.

1. The Information We Collect About You And How We Collect It.

This privacy policy covers our use of information that can or could be used to identify you ("Personal Information"). We need to collect and use certain Personal Information to provide the Services to you and fulfill the promises we make to you in the Terms of Service. More details about how we use the information is found below.

The information we collect falls into three groups: (1) the information that you may choose to voluntarily provide to us while using our Services (described below under "Information You Provide to Us" and (2) information collected automatically as a result of your use of our Services (described below under "Information Collected Automatically"); and (3) information collected from third parties (described below under "Information Collected From Others").

Information You Provide To Us

There are several circumstances in which you may choose to voluntarily provide certain Personal Information to us. For example:

When you sign up for our Services, you give us Personal Information voluntarily by providing it to us. That information includes but is not limited to the username you choose to use for our services, your name, address, and your Dental license number or DEA number.

You may also voluntarily give us Personal Information when you make a purchase through us, subscribe to our emails, newsletters, enter promotions, or communicate with us. Depending on which of these Services you use, that may include your name, address, phone number, email address, Dental license number or DEA number, and your product purchase/return/exchange information.

You may also voluntarily provide us with Personal Information if you fill out a form on our Site that requests such information (e.g., submitting a Take Down Request).

You may also voluntarily provide us with Personal Information when you send an email message to any Dentaklik.com email address published on our Site or when you use the Chat feature of our Site. When you use such features, we retain the contents of your messages or chats, your email address, and any information you provide to us through those communications such as your name, your contact preferences, and any response we send back to you.

Dentaklik engages with customers on multiple social media platforms (e.g., Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn). If you contact us on one of our social media platforms, request customer service via social media or otherwise direct us to communicate with you via social media, we may contact you via direct message or use other social media tools to interact with you. Your interactions with us are governed by this privacy policy as well as the privacy policy of the social media platform you use.

When you make a purchase using our Services, you will be required to provide a valid credit card number or other payment information. Currently, our payment processor is Stripe. Stripe uses and processes your payment information in accordance with its own privacy policy. We encourage you to read Stripe's policy. Importantly, neither Dentaklik nor its vendors will collect, use, or store the credit card number you provide when making a purchase on our site.

There may be other circumstances in which you chose to give us Personal Information. However, we ask that you never send us any highly sensitive Personal Information (such as protected health data or Social Security numbers) through our Site.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most web-based services, we (or our service providers) may automatically collect Personal Information from your browser or your device when you use the Site. For example we may collect and use your IP address, browser type, operating system, location information, the web page you were visiting before accessing our Services, the pages on our Site which you browsed or the features you used, and the time spent on those pages or features, the links on our Site that you click on, device and advertising identifiers, and your purchase history to understand who is using our Services and how.

Cookies and Other Information

Dentaklik use several different automated technologies to operate our Site, communicate with our customers, and ensure we are effective in our advertising. For example, we use "cookies," which are small text files that are placed on your computer or device by a Web server when you access our Services. We may place and access cookies and similar technologies on your computer or device, which are known as "first party cookies." Cookies may also be placed and accessed by third parties, which are known as "third party cookies" and are described below.

We use cookies to monitor aggregate usage and web traffic of our Services, customize and improve our Services, help you navigate our Services, deliver content specific to your interests and deliver advertisements to you.

We also use "web beacons" (also known as web bugs, pixel tags, or clear GIFS) which are small graphics with a unique identifier that may be included on our Services for example to deliver or communicate with cookies, to track and measure the performance of our Services, to monitor how many visitors view our Services, and to monitor the effectiveness of our advertising. Unlike cookies, which are stored on a user's hard drive or device, web beacons are typically embedded invisibly on web pages (or in an email).

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These are cookies that are essential to enable you to use the Services, for example moving around our websites and using their features (e.g. cookies that enable you to log into the Services).
  • Performance Cookies: These are cookies which collect information about how and when you and other visitors interact with our Services (e.g. pages viewed most often) and the information collected by these cookies is used to improve how our Services work.
  • Functionality Cookies: These are cookies that allow our Services to remember choices you made (such as your login information) and provide you with content and features which are customized to you.
  • Advertising Cookies: These are cookies which are used to deliver advertisements to you about products available from Dentaklik which are more relevant to you and your interests. We may also share this type of information with third parties for these purposes.

The types of cookies described above may be "session cookies" or "persistent cookies." Session cookies are only stored on your device whilst you are using the Services, and are deleted when you log off from the Services or close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a period of time after you log off from the Services or close your browser.

Third Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third parties when you use the Services as some third party service providers that we engage (including third party advertisers) may place their own cookies on your hard drive or device. These cookies may provide information to those third parties about your browsing habits. Some also help to serve you with advertisements which are more relevant to you.

For example, our websites use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, Inc. ("Google"). Google Analytics uses non-operational cookies, which are text files placed on your computer to help the website analyse how visitors use the website. The information generated by the cookie about your use of the website (including your IP address) will be transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States. At our request, Google will use this information for the purpose of evaluating how the website is used, compiling reports on website activity for website operators and providing other services relating to website activity and internet usage. Google may also transfer this information to third parties where required to do so by law, or where such third parties process the information on Google's behalf. You can opt-out of Google Analytics for Display Advertising and customize Google Display Network ads using the Ads Settings Feature on Google. By clicking Ads Settings, you will be taken out of this Website and to Google where you can control the information Google uses to show you ads. In addition, you can use Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on to disable tracking by Google Analytics. You can also find more information about Google Analytics here.

Similarly, we use the Facebook pixel service. The Facebook pixel is an analytics tool that allows us to measure the effectiveness of our advertising by understanding the actions you take on other websites. We use it to make sure our advertising on Facebook is shown to the right people and is effective. When you visit our website and take an action, this triggers the pixel which reports this action to Facebook. You may opt out of the collection and use of your information for ad targeting by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance's Consumer Choice Tool.

Dentaklik reserves its rights to use other third party cookies or tools not listed here in this Policy. Should you wish to know the cookies being used on our Site at any particular time, we encourage you to use free tools such as Ghostery that will provide you with a list of all cookies as well as the ability to block or prevent them from being used.

Cookies Settings

Although most browsers automatically accept cookies, you can change your browser options to stop automatically accepting cookies or to prompt you before accepting cookies. However, if you don't accept cookies, you may not be able to access all portions or features of the Services.

While you may disable the usage of cookies through your browser settings, Dentaklik currently does not respond to a "Do Not Track" signal in the HTTP header from your browser or mobile application due to the lack of industry standard on how to interpret that signal.

Information Collected From Others

In addition to the information you provide to us and the information we collect automatically, we collect data that's publicly available. For example, information you submit in a public forum (e.g., a blog, chat room or social network) can be read, collected or used by us and others, and could be used to personalize your experience. You are responsible for the information you choose to submit in these instances.

We also obtain information provided by third parties. For instance, we obtain information from companies that can enhance our existing customer information to improve the accuracy and add to the information we have about our customers (e.g., adding address information).

12. The California Consumer Protection Act ("CCPA")

The following section on CCPA, Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100, applies to California residents who have certain rights regarding the personal information (as defined in the CCPA) collected by Dentaklik. It does not cover personal information not subject to the CCPA rights identified below, such as personal information we collect from our employees or job applicants. Likewise, the CCPA does not extend all the rights identified below to personal information collected from employees, owners, directors, officers, or contractors of businesses in the course of our provision or receipt of business-related services.

Definitions
  • "Personal information" or "PI" is information that identifies, relates to, describes, is capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular consumer or household.
  • "Consumer" is a California resident, a natural person who is a California resident for other than a temporary or transitory purposes, or someone who is domiciled in California but is currently outside the State for a temporary or transitory purpose.
  • "Sale" means selling, renting, releasing, disclosing, disseminating, making available, transferring, or otherwise communicating orally, in writing, or by electronic or other means, a consumer’s personal information by Dentaklik to another business or a third party for monetary or other valuable consideration.
California residents have the following rights under CCPA
  • Right to Opt Out. You have the right, at any time, to direct a company to not sell your PI. At this time, Dentaklik does not sell PI.
  • Right to Know or Right to be Informed. Subject to certain exceptions, you have the right to know what specific pieces of PI Dentaklik has collected about you in the 12 months preceding your request, along with the categories of sources of that PI, our business or commercial purpose for disclosing your PI, and the categories of third parties with whom we share your PI. You may make this request no more than twice per 12-month period.
  • Right to Request Deletion. You have the right to request deletion of the PI Dentaklik has collected from you, subject to certain exceptions under the CCPA including, without limitation:
    • a. Making sure you can receive products you have purchased if you would still like to,
    • b. Making sure deletion does not affect your obligations to suppliers,
    • c. Complying with Dentaklik's legal or compliance obligations,
    • d. Detecting fraudulent behavior or security incidents, and
    • e. Other reasons that are in connection with our lawful internal uses that are consistent with your relationship with us and the context in which the information was collected.
Exercising Your Rights under CCPA

You can exercise any of your rights under CCPA by going to Dentaklik.com/ccpa-compliance-form or by emailing us at support@dentaklik.com.

We will take reasonable steps to verify your identity before responding to a request depending on the type of request you make.

  • Verification needed if you exercise your Right to Delete or a similar right that does not require the disclosure of specific pieces of information about you:
    • a. f you are writing to us from the email address associated with your Dentaklik account or transactions that Dentaklik has on file for you, then to complete the verification process, we will ask you to confirm the action you wish us to take before we proceed (such as confirming you would like us to delete your information).
    • b. If you are not writing to us from the email address associated with your Dentaklik account, then to complete the verification process, we will ask you to provide 3 specific pieces of data that we have on file for you, such as email address you used when making a transaction or billing information such as the last 4 digits of the credit card used or the billing address for a transaction.
  • Verification needed if you exercise your Right to Know or a similar right that does require disclosure of specific pieces of information about you:
    • a. If you are writing to us from the email address associated with your Dentaklik account or transactions that Dentaklik has on file for you, then to complete the verification process, we will ask you to both: 1) confirm the action you wish us to take before we proceed and 2) also provide a statement that you "declare under penalty of perjury that you are the person about whom the Right to Know request has been made."
    • b. If you are not writing to us from the email address associated with your Dentaklik account, then to complete the verification process, we will ask you to both: 1) provide 3 specific pieces of data that we have on file for you, such as email address you used when making a transaction or billing information such as the last 4 digits of the credit card used or the billing address for a transaction and 2) also provide a statement that you "declare under penalty of perjury that you are the person about whom the Right to Know request has been made."
  • You are also permitted to designate an authorized agent to submit certain requests on your behalf. In order for an authorized agent to be verified, you must provide the authorized agent with signed, written permission to make such requests or a power of attorney. We may also follow up with you to verify your identity according to the above methods before processing the authorized agent’s request.
  • If we are not able to successfully verify you according to the above methods, then we will not be able to comply with your request.

Dentaklik will not discriminate against you if you exercise your rights under CCPA. However, if your request requires us to delete, restrict or not share information, please be advised that this will inhibit some functionality of the Dentaklik's site. Any subsequent interaction with Dentaklik's site after deletion will require new requests for action on your data.
